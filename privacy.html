MANGALAUNCH / YOUR TRUST MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Your reading life should stay yours. This policy explains what data is used, where it goes and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

At a glance

  • Your library and reading progress are stored on your device by default.
  • Google Drive sync and third-party connections are optional.
  • For any question or privacy request, email mangalaunch@proton.me.

Who we are and what this covers

This policy applies to the MangaLaunch application and its public website. “We” means the MangaLaunch project. For support, privacy, security or any other question, contact mangalaunch@proton.me.

Third-party websites, extension publishers, trackers, hosting and cloud services process information under their own policies. This policy describes our use of data and does not replace theirs.

Reading data and local storage

The app stores the information needed for your reading experience: library entries, categories, personal ratings, chapter and page progress, read/unread marks, history, favorites, saved source positions, statistics, preferences and downloaded chapters.

Source sign-in sessions and connected-service credentials are stored on the device as needed for the features you enable. These credentials and downloaded chapter files are not included in Google Drive synchronization. Network services you choose to use still receive the requests needed to provide their content.

Optional Google Drive synchronization

Google Drive is connected only when you authorize it. MangaLaunch uses a dedicated hidden app-data folder in your own Drive to read, write and delete synchronization records and backups. This connection does not grant access to your other Drive documents. Google may also provide your account email and display name so the app can identify the connected account.

You choose which modules to sync: library, categories, category assignments, personal ratings, current chapter and page, read/unread chapters, history, saved source positions, favorite chapters and reading statistics. Sync records also include an app-generated device identifier, device label, platform and timestamps to coordinate changes and show connected devices.

Google API data is used for these requested synchronization and backup features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Our handling and transfer of Google API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google processes information under its Privacy Policy.

Sources, translation and other connections

When you browse a source, read online or sign in on its website, that service receives requests and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. It may set its own cookies. Connected trackers receive the account and reading updates required for the features you use.

On Android, page text can be recognized on the device and sent to an external translation service when you enable translation. The translation service processes that text under its own terms; translation is optional.

Unofficial extensions may have their own publishers, network access and data practices. Review the publisher, permissions and source policies before installing. Installing an unofficial extension is your choice and your risk; see the Terms of Service for the responsibility limits.

The website and messages to us

The website remembers your language choice in browser local storage. The current site does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process connection and request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email mangalaunch@proton.me, we receive your email address, message and any files or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate a reported issue and protect the service. Please do not send passwords or private keys. Our email provider processes messages under the Proton Privacy Policy.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can turn off optional connections, choose sync modules and manage reading data through the app and your device. Clearing browser storage removes the website's saved language. Keep your own backup before deleting data.

Disconnecting Google Drive or revoking access in your Google Account stops future authorized access; it does not itself remove copies already stored in Drive or on another device. Delete cloud app data separately through Google Drive's app-management settings. Reconnecting another device may restore data that still exists there.

Local data remains until you remove it. Cloud data remains until deleted from the cloud or removed under the provider's rules. We retain support messages only as needed to handle the request, security and legal obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion of information we hold by emailing mangalaunch@proton.me. Depending on applicable law, you may also have objection, portability and complaint rights. We cannot directly erase files stored only on your device or in an account you control.

Protection, sharing and age

We do not sell personal information or use reading data for targeted advertising. Data is shared with services you choose as needed for their features, with providers handling hosting or support, or when necessary to meet legal obligations and protect users. These providers may process data in other countries.

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections for Google Drive and the storage protections available on your platform. No system can guarantee complete security. MangaLaunch is not directed at children under 13; a higher minimum age may apply where you live. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the current text. Where required, we will provide an additional notice or ask for authorization before using data for a new purpose.

All questions, support, privacy and security requests: mangalaunch@proton.me. You can also read our Terms of Service.
