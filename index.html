begin.

What is MangaLaunch?+

An app for manga, manhwa and webtoons with a personal library, reading history, chapter downloads and optional sources. It brings your reading together.

Where do the titles come from?+

Sources come in separate extensions. Choose a package, review its publisher and enable the sources you want. Catalog features and chapter access depend on the source.

Can I read offline?+

Yes. Download chapters in the app ahead of time, then read them offline.

Which devices are supported?+

MangaLaunch works on Windows and Android, with layouts for desktop and mobile. Some features differ by platform: page translation is available in the mobile app.

Is this the actual app interface?+

The interactive mockups illustrate MangaLaunch features. The story titles and library covers shown here are fictional examples.
