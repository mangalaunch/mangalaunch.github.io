# MangaLaunch

**Your manga. Your rhythm.**

MangaLaunch brings manga, manhwa and webtoons into one personal reading space. Keep your favorite stories together, discover something new and pick up where you left off — on Windows or Android.

## A library that feels like yours

Organize titles into your own categories, keep track of what you are reading and save stories for later. Personal ratings, reading history and statistics help you follow your journey through every chapter.

## Read your way

Switch between page-by-page reading and continuous webtoon scrolling. Adjust the theme, zoom, reading direction and title-specific preferences to suit your screen and your habits. MangaLaunch remembers your current chapter and page, so you can return to the story without searching for your place.

## Take your stories with you

Download chapters ahead of time and read offline. Whether you are at home, on a commute or away from a reliable connection, saved chapters stay ready to read.

## Explore the catalogs you choose

Connect optional extensions to browse different sources from one app. Search for titles, use each source's filters, explore recommendations and save your position in a catalog. Sign in to a source when it requires an account. Available content and features depend on the source.

## A little help with another language

The Android app offers page translation, helping you follow stories in another language while staying in the reader.

## Continue across devices

Use your personal Google Drive to keep your library and reading progress in sync. Choose which parts of your reading life to share between devices, including categories, personal ratings, history, favorites and saved catalog positions. Backups and conflict handling help you stay in control of your data.

## Keep your reading journey connected

Connect AniList or MyAnimeList to track your progress in a service you already use. Your reading history and statistics also remain available inside MangaLaunch.
